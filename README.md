# Activation & Retention Analysis — Uno Wallet

**For:** Uno Wallet — AI Business Analyst (YC P26), New York / Remote
**Author:** Bhupesh Chandra Dimri
**Stack:** Python 3, pandas, scikit-learn, matplotlib

## One-liner
A wallet lives or dies on whether users come back after week one. This project
measures activation and retention on synthetic user-event data, ranks what
actually predicts D30 retention, and designs the randomised test that would
prove whether *causing* activation *causes* retention.

## ⚠️ Synthetic data — read first
All user data in this project is **synthetic**, generated by `src/data_gen.py`
with a known, auditable process. **Nothing here is real Uno Wallet user data**
and no result should be read as a claim about Uno Wallet's real product,
users, or performance. The generator's drivers are documented in the code so
the analysis can be checked against ground truth.

## Headline results (2,400 synthetic users, 12 signup weeks)
- **Activation 56.3%** (first successful optimised payment within 7 days),
  **D7 retention 30.0%**, **D30 retention 19.8%**, **D60 retention 11.0%**.
- D30 retention runs from **6.6%** (not activated, 0 cards linked) to **35.6%**
  (activated, 3 cards linked).
- **Honest finding:** the simple two-feature rule (activated AND ≥2 cards
  linked) beats both fitted models on F1 (0.443 vs 0.436 logistic, 0.421
  random forest) and trails by only 0.05–0.06 AUC. The models add ranking
  value (lift @ top 20%: 1.87–1.94× vs 1.58× for the rule) — not decision value.
- **Correlation ≠ causation:** referral users retain best (D30 23.8% vs paid
  15.5%), but they also link more cards (1.80 vs 1.13). Channel looks like a
  driver until you condition on cards linked. Only the randomised test in
  §9 can establish causality.
- **Experiment:** the 48-hour activation nudge, powered for +3pp on D30,
  needs **2,915 users per arm — 30 weeks** at 200 signups/week. The pragmatic
  fallback (+5pp) needs 1,084 per arm, 11 weeks. That timeline is itself a
  finding: a 2-person startup cannot afford a 3pp MDE.

## Definitions (used consistently everywhere)
- **Activation:** first successful optimised payment within 7 days of signup.
- **D7 retention:** at least one transaction on days 5–7 after signup.
- **D30 retention:** at least one transaction on days 28–30 after signup.
- **D60 retention:** at least one transaction on days 58–60 after signup.
- All model features are known at the end of week 1 — nothing peeks at the future.
